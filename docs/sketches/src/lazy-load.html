Initial load: the map loads with the page. Load on scroll: nothing of the map loads until it scrolls into view. Load on click: the page shows a placeholder with a button, and the map loads when the visitor clicks it. While a map waits, none of its scripts or requests load, and a search made meanwhile is shown once it does.
